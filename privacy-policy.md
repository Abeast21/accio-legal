# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Overview
Accio ("the app") is a personal account manager that helps you remember which accounts you've used for different apps and services. We take your privacy seriously.

## What we collect
- **Account information**: Your email address and password (encrypted) when you sign up
- **App data**: The account entries and linked apps you create inside Accio
- **Usage data**: Basic app usage analytics to improve the experience

## What we don't collect
- We do not collect your actual passwords for third-party apps
- We do not sell your data to advertisers or third parties
- We do not track your location

## How we store your data
Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen.

## Third-party services
Accio uses the following third-party services:
- **Supabase** — for authentication and database storage (supabase.com/privacy)

## Your rights
- You can export your data at any time from the Profile screen
- You can delete your account and all data permanently from the Profile screen
- You can contact us to request data deletion or modification

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
